BloodLine doesn't diagnose, decide urgency, automate follow-up, or replace your inbox review or the lab's phone call.

It just makes sure you know.

Why now.

1 July 2026

Patients can see results first.

Pathology reports now go to My Health Record by default, mostly without the old 7‑day delay. A patient may read a result before anyone at the practice has acted on it.

26 August 2026

Follow‑up is an accreditation standard.

The RACGP's 6th edition Standards keep a dedicated criterion for follow‑up systems: CG6.

Late 2025

Labs have started texting doctors.

Australian Clinical Labs texts doctors about high‑risk results and asks them to confirm. It covers one lab, judged by that lab's own criteria.

Now

One connection reaches most practices.

Halo Connect is the only connection method for Bp Omni, and Bp Premier integrations are moving to it.

Made for the practice you already run.

Plugs in where results already land.

Labs change nothing. BloodLine's first integration route is Best Practice, through Halo Connect.

No patient details in messages.

The same answer, every time.

Your judgement stays yours.
