    What changes from the demo

    PartDemo todayProduction plan
    Results inSynthetic PDFs from the simulator or an uploadPractice software through Halo Connect, structured data first
    Reading reportsOCR through OpenRouter, providers outside AustraliaA secure, Australian-hosted OCR service, as a fallback only
    StorageSQLite file and report folder on the laptopEncrypted Postgres in an Australian region, split by practice
    Sign-inPick a GP, no passwordReal sign-in with MFA, limited to the GP's practice
    Alerts to the phoneApp polls every 4 seconds while openPush notifications through Firebase
    ProcessingInside the requestA job queue with retries
    Email, SMS, callsResend and Twilio, simulated unless switched onOur own email domain and an Australian sender number, with delivery tracking
    HostingA laptop and a Cloudflare quick tunnelAustralian hosting, with monitoring and alerting
